# Orders for events

Fictional document: Délices d'Awa is an imaginary business, created for “Mastering Claude”, Volume 2.

## When is it an event?

An order of at least 30 bottles for a wedding, a baptism, a birthday or a company party is treated as an event. Beyond 50 bottles, even without an event, the price is on quote.

## Lead time

Order at least 7 days before the date of the event. For more than 200 bottles, allow 14 days: Awa must reserve the fruit with her growers.

## Quote and deposit

Awa sends a quote within 24 hours. The order is confirmed when a 50% deposit is paid, by Mobile Money. The balance is paid on delivery. The deposit is not refunded if the event is canceled less than 48 hours before delivery.

## Personalized labels

For events of more than 100 bottles, Awa can have a label printed with the couple's first names or the company's name, on quote, with a lead time of 10 days.

## Event delivery

Event orders are delivered in Cotonou, to the venue of the party, at the time agreed with Awa, including on Sunday if the quote provides for it.
