# Frequently asked questions

Fictional document: Délices d'Awa is an imaginary business, created for “Mastering Claude”, Volume 2.

## How do I place an order?

Send a WhatsApp message to Délices d'Awa, Monday to Saturday, from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. Give the products, the quantities, your name and your neighborhood. The Comptoir, our assistant, prepares a summary with the total: the order is recorded only after your confirmation.

## Are you open on Sundays?

No. On Sundays, the workshop is closed and there is no delivery. Messages received on Sunday are handled on Monday from 8 a.m.

## When will I be delivered?

Any order confirmed before 12 p.m. is delivered the same day, in the afternoon slot. After 12 p.m., it is delivered the next day, except on Sunday: an order confirmed on Saturday after 12 p.m. is delivered on Monday.

## Do I pay for delivery?

Delivery is free from 5 bottles in the same order. Below that, it costs 500 FCFA. Bottles of syrup do not count as bottles for free delivery.

## Do you deliver outside Cotonou?

No, we deliver only in Cotonou. Customers from Abomey-Calavi, Porto-Novo or elsewhere can pick up their order at the workshop in Fidjrossè, Monday to Saturday, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

## How do I pay?

By Mobile Money, MTN MoMo or Moov Money, or in cash on delivery. For a Mobile Money payment, Awa sends you the instructions after the order is confirmed. We do not accept bank cards.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, as long as the order has not left for delivery. Write to us on WhatsApp: the Comptoir prepares a new summary, which replaces the old one. Once the order is on its way, Awa decides case by case.

## What should I do if a bottle is damaged or the order is wrong?

Report it on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo if possible. Awa looks at each complaint and answers you herself within 24 hours.

## Is there a deposit on the bottles?

No, there is no paid deposit. Our bottles are glass and reusable: give them back clean to the rider at the next delivery. We wash and sterilize them before filling them again.

## How long does a juice keep?

Our juices are fresh, with no preservatives. Closed, they keep 5 days in the refrigerator, between 2 and 6 °C. Once opened, drink them within 48 hours. The use-by date is written on the cap.

## How long does a syrup keep?

Closed, a bottle of syrup keeps 3 months away from heat and light. Once opened, keep it in the refrigerator and use it within the month.

## How do I use the syrup?

Count one measure of syrup for six measures of cool water. Ginger syrup also mixes with sparkling water or tea.

## Do your juices contain sugar?

Pineapple juice contains no added sugar. Bissap, ginger and soursop juices are sweetened with cane sugar. The exact quantities are in the product sheets.

## Do you give discounts?

Prices are those of the catalog. Only Awa can grant a special price, for example for a large quantity: the Comptoir passes the request on to her. Beyond 50 bottles, the price is on quote.

## Do you offer other flavors?

Our juices are bissap, ginger, pineapple and soursop, and our syrups bissap and ginger. We have no other flavor for the moment. Seasonal juices are announced on our WhatsApp status.

## Can I visit the workshop?

The workshop in Fidjrossè is not open to visits, for hygiene reasons. You can pick up an order there Monday to Saturday, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

## How do I reach Awa?

The simplest way is to write to Délices d'Awa's WhatsApp: the Comptoir passes on to Awa everything that needs her decision. We do not give out a personal number.
