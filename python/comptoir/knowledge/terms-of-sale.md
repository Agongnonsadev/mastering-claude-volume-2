# Terms of sale

Fictional document: Délices d'Awa is an imaginary business, created for “Mastering Claude”, Volume 2. Version of October 1, 2026.

## Orders

Orders are placed through WhatsApp, Monday to Saturday, from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. An order is recorded only after the customer's confirmation, in reply to the official summary sent by the Comptoir. The summary gives the products, the quantities, the delivery fee and the total.

## Prices

Prices are in CFA francs, all taxes included. Bissap, ginger, pineapple and soursop juice: 1,000 FCFA for a 500 ml bottle. Bissap and ginger syrups: 2,500 FCFA a bottle. The price applied is that of the day of the confirmation.

## Delivery

Délices d'Awa delivers only in Cotonou, Monday to Saturday. An order confirmed before 12 p.m. is delivered the same day, between 4 and 7 p.m.; after 12 p.m., it is delivered the next day between 10 a.m. and 1 p.m., or on Monday if the next day is a Sunday. Delivery is free from 5 bottles, and costs 500 FCFA below that. Outside Cotonou, the order can be picked up at the workshop in Fidjrossè.

## Payment

Payment is made by Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Moov Money) before delivery, or in cash on delivery. Bank cards are not accepted.

## Complaints

Any complaint, damaged product, wrong order or delay, is made on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo if possible. Awa looks at each case and answers within 24 hours. She alone decides what happens next: replacement, credit or another solution.

## Cancellation

An order can be changed or canceled free of charge as long as it has not left for delivery. For events, the deposit is not refunded less than 48 hours before the delivery date.

## Personal data

Délices d'Awa keeps the name, WhatsApp number and delivery neighborhood of its customers to prepare and deliver the orders. Conversations with the Comptoir are deleted after 30 days. A customer can ask at any time for their data to be deleted, through WhatsApp.
