# Product sheets

Fictional document: Délices d'Awa is an imaginary business, created for “Mastering Claude”, Volume 2. The quantities are those of the workshop's recipes.

## Bissap juice

500 ml glass bottle, 1,000 FCFA. Ingredients: infusion of dried hibiscus flowers, filtered water, cane sugar, fresh mint, natural vanilla flavoring. Added sugar: 30 g per bottle. Best served very cold.

## Ginger juice

500 ml glass bottle, 1,000 FCFA. Ingredients: grated fresh ginger, filtered water, lime juice, cane sugar. Added sugar: 28 g per bottle. Spicy taste: shake before serving, the ginger settles at the bottom.

## Pineapple juice

500 ml glass bottle, 1,000 FCFA. Ingredients: pressed sugarloaf pineapple, with no added water or sugar. The sugar comes from the fruit itself. Harvested in Benin, pressed on the day of bottling.

## Soursop juice

500 ml glass bottle, 1,000 FCFA. Ingredients: soursop pulp, filtered water, cane sugar, a pinch of nutmeg. Added sugar: 25 g per bottle. Thick texture: shake before serving. Soursop depends on deliveries: it may be missing on some days.

## Bissap syrup

500 ml glass bottle, 2,500 FCFA. Ingredients: concentrated infusion of hibiscus flowers, cane sugar, lemon juice. To be diluted: one measure of syrup for six measures of water.

## Ginger syrup

500 ml glass bottle, 2,500 FCFA. Ingredients: fresh ginger, cane sugar, lime juice. To be diluted: one measure of syrup for six measures of water, still or sparkling.

## Allergens

None of our recipes contains any of the 14 major allergens: no gluten, no milk, no tree nuts, no peanuts. The workshop handles none of these products.

## Health

Our juices and syrups are drinks, not remedies. We give no health advice. For any question about your diet, diabetes or pregnancy for example, ask a health professional; the quantities of sugar given above can help them.
