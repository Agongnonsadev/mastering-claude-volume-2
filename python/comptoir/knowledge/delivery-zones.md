# Delivery zones and slots

Fictional document: Délices d'Awa is an imaginary business, created for “Mastering Claude”, Volume 2.

## Neighborhoods served

Délices d'Awa delivers throughout Cotonou. Examples of neighborhoods served: Akpakpa, Cadjèhoun, Fidjrossè, Gbégamey, Zogbo, Haie Vive, Agla, Ganhi, Jéricho, Vèdoko, Saint-Michel. If your neighborhood is not in this list, tell us: if it is in Cotonou, we deliver.

## Outside Cotonou

Godomey and Togbin belong to the municipality of Abomey-Calavi: we do not deliver there, as with Porto-Novo and the other municipalities. Customers from these municipalities can pick up their order at the workshop in Fidjrossè, Monday to Saturday, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

## Slots

Two delivery slots, Monday to Saturday: from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., for orders confirmed the day before after 12 p.m.; from 4 to 7 p.m., for orders confirmed the same day before 12 p.m. The rider calls 15 minutes before arriving.

## Rainy days

On days of heavy rain, deliveries may be delayed or postponed to the next day. The Comptoir warns the customers concerned as soon as Awa decides so.
